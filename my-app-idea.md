# BlueHen CarPool

## One-Sentence Summary

BlueHen CarPool is a carpool community for University of Delaware students in Newark that helps students who need rides connect with student drivers who have available seats and are traveling in the same direction.

## The Problem

Many University of Delaware students do not have convenient access to a car. Some students, especially those living on campus, may not bring a car because parking can be expensive or inconvenient. However, they may still need transportation to places such as grocery stores, shopping malls, Philadelphia, New York City, Washington D.C., or their hometowns.

At the same time, many students with cars already make these trips and may have empty seats. Currently, students may try to find rides through friends, group chats, or social media, but this information can be unorganized and difficult to search.

BlueHen CarPool provides a community where UD students can post ride offers or ride requests and connect with other students who are traveling in the same direction.

## User Types

- Riders: UD students who are looking for a ride.
- Drivers: UD students who have a car and can offer available seats on a trip.
- A user can be both a rider and a driver depending on the trip.

## Core Features

- Create a ride offer or ride request with the destination, date, time, number of seats or passengers, and price.
- Browse available ride offers and ride requests posted by other UD students.
- Request a seat from a driver or respond to a rider's ride request.
- Accept or decline ride requests and view confirmed trips.
- Update or delete a user's own ride posts.

## What Users See

### Screen 1: Home / Ride Board

Users can browse current ride offers and ride requests from the UD community. Each post shows basic information such as the starting point, destination, date, departure time, available seats, and price.

### Screen 2: Create a Ride

Users choose whether they are offering a ride or looking for a ride. They enter information such as the starting point, destination, date, time, number of seats or passengers, price, and additional notes.

Drivers can also provide basic information about their vehicle and indicate information about their driver's license and insurance.

### Screen 3: Ride Details

Users can open a ride to see more information about the trip and the student who posted it. Riders can request an available seat, and drivers can respond to ride requests.

### Screen 4: My Trips

Users can see the rides they posted, rides they requested, and confirmed trips. They can also update or cancel their own ride posts.

## Example Scenario

Liam is a University of Delaware student in Newark who wants to travel to New York City on Friday evening but does not want to drive.

He opens BlueHen CarPool and searches the ride board. He finds another UD student who is already driving from Newark to New York City on Friday at 5:30 PM. The driver has two available seats and is asking for $25 per passenger.

Liam opens the trip, reviews the trip and driver information, and requests one seat. The driver sees Liam's request and accepts it. The trip then appears as confirmed in both users' "My Trips" pages.

The app helps Liam find transportation while allowing the driver to fill an empty seat and earn some money toward the cost and effort of the trip.
